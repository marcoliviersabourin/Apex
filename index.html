🎯 Opportunity Radar
How to read this table
Scans all tickers (holdings + watchlist + ideas) for potential opportunities using three dimensions: Value (is it cheap?), Quality (does it deserve to recover?), and Bottom Signal (is the reversal starting?). Works in ALL regimes — not just crisis. A dislocated quality stock in a trending market is an opportunity too.
How to act by regime:
TRENDING: Trust BX signals. TURN + high value = buy with normal sizing. VOLATILE: Require confirmation. Wait for fundamental enrichment before acting. CRISIS: DCA approach. Don't go all-in on first signal. Scale in over 2-3 months.
SCORES:
Value (0-6): Price vs high, forward P/E vs sector, FCF yield, revenue growth
Quality (0-3): Core tag, analyst not EXIT, already vetted
Bottom (0-5): BX TURN signal, recent BUY, decelerating decline
STATUS:
★ STRONG BUY Score ≥ 10 — deeply undervalued + bottom forming
ACTIONABLE Score 7-9 — good setup, consider entering
WATCH Score 4-6 — potential, wait for more confirmation
Below 4 = not shown
